Gomoku

Finished

Five in a row on a 15×15 board, played against a computer opponent that explains its moves. It's a standalone Python and Pygame course project; all graphics are drawn in code.

Play it right here. Click an intersection to place a stone, or use the arrow keys and press Enter. Connect five or more in any direction to win.

AI's last decision

This is a JavaScript version of the Python game with the same rules and AI, so it can run in your browser. The orange ring marks the latest stone. While playing, press U to undo your turn and R for a new game.

  • Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty, and you choose your stone color.
  • Undo your last turn, even while the AI is thinking, and review the board after a game.
  • A live AI panel shows how deep the search went, how many positions it visited, and how long it took.

How the AI works. It uses classic game AI, not machine learning. It first checks for winning moves and forced blocks, then scores nearby moves by recognizing patterns like open threes and fours. Finally it searches ahead with negamax and alpha-beta pruning, getting deeper each round until it runs out of time.

Coin Catchers

Finished

A 2D platformer in Unity with three levels. Run, jump, climb ladders, shoot enemies, and collect coins on the way to each level's exit.

  • A title screen, lives, and sound effects.
  • Enemies, coin pickups, ladders, and shooting, built from my own C# scripts.
  • Controls: A/D or arrow keys to move, W/S to climb, Space to jump, and left click or Enter to shoot.

Sprites come from Super Platformer Assets by Foxfin, supplied through GameDev.tv.

  • Unity 2022.3
  • C#
  • Universal Render Pipeline

UHD ACM 2D Platformer

Work in progress

A multiplayer 2D platformer we're building as a team in UHD's ACM club. Players join one game together, vote on a map, and build the level as they play by placing and rotating pieces on a grid.

My part so far:

  • A shared camera that follows one player or zooms out to keep every player in view.
  • Rotating placeable pieces with the R key while dragging them in build mode.
  • Host-controlled player movement and ladder climbing over the network.
  • Map voting and host-controlled multiplayer rounds.
  • Fixes to scene loading and the multiplayer startup scenes.
  • Unity 6
  • C#
  • Photon Fusion
